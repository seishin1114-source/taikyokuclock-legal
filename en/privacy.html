Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026 · 日本語

Miyajin Product ("we", "us") sets out below how information is handled in the iOS app "Game Clock" (Japanese name: 対局時計) (the "App").

The App has no server of ours and no account registration. The only things stored on your device are your settings, such as your last time control, and your game records (player names, results, time used on each move and so on). Game records are stored only on your device and are never sent anywhere.

1. Information handled

  1. Settings: the time control you chose last (preset, or the type, minutes, seconds and so on that you set yourself), whether sounds and the voice are on, and the appearance setting. Stored only on your device.
  2. Game records: for games that end on time or with End Game (after choosing the result while paused): player names (if entered), date and time, time control, result and reason, and the time used on each move. The player names you entered last are also remembered for the next game. All of this is stored only on your device and is never sent.
  3. Purchase status: whether you have purchased "Remove Ads". Purchases and payments are handled by Apple; we never receive your payment information. The last result is remembered on your device so that no ads are requested right after launch.
  4. Advertising identifiers, etc.: to deliver ads, ad providers may collect the IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) and information about your device and usage (Section 5).

The App does not ask for or collect your email address or location. Entering player names is optional, and nicknames are fine. Games for which you choose "Don't Record", and games that you reset or leave before the end, are not recorded.

2. Purposes of use

The information is used to (a) remember your last settings so you can start the next game right away, (b) show the results and time usage of your games so you can look back at them, (c) provide "Remove Ads", (d) fix problems, and (e) deliver, optimize and measure ads.

3. Where data is stored

InformationStored in
SettingsOn your device
Game recordsOn your device only (never sent)
Purchase statusApple (App Store), checked on your device; the last result is remembered on your device

Nothing is sent to or stored on our servers. The App does not sync with iCloud, but if device backups (such as iCloud Backup) are turned on, the settings and game records on your device may be included in those backups.

4. Information sent outside the device

  1. Google AdMob: advertising identifiers and similar information may be sent to deliver ads (Section 5).
  2. Apple (App Store): the App communicates with Apple to purchase and restore "Remove Ads".

The clock, sounds, haptics and voice work entirely on your device, and game records are stored only on your device; none of them are sent anywhere. We do not sell or provide your information to third parties except as required by law.

5. Advertising and tracking (ATT)

  1. The App shows a Google AdMob banner ad only at the bottom of the home screen (where you choose a time control). No ads are shown on the clock screen during a game, on the result screen, or in the menu. There are no full-screen ads.
  2. If you allow tracking in iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT), the advertising identifier (IDFA) may be used for personalized ads. If you do not allow it, non-personalized ads are shown and you can still use the App.
  3. SKAdNetwork and similar mechanisms may be used to measure ad performance.
  4. In the EEA, the UK and other applicable regions, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) is shown, and no ads are requested until consent is obtained. You can change your choice at any time from the menu (≡) at the top right of the home screen → "Ad Privacy Settings".
  5. If you purchase "Remove Ads", no ads are shown and the ad components are not started.

6. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob (ad delivery) / Google User Messaging Platform (consent management)
  • Apple (App Store, In-App Purchase)

Please see each company's privacy policy for how they handle information (Google Privacy Policy / Google advertising / Apple Privacy Policy).

7. Retention and deletion

Settings and game records on your device are erased when you delete the App. You can also delete game records one by one in Game Records in the menu (swipe left on a row). Because we keep no copy, we cannot restore records that have been erased. A "Remove Ads" purchase can be brought back after reinstalling with "Restore Purchases".

8. Your rights

Depending on the laws where you live (such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your information. Because we keep no user information on servers, information on your device can be erased by deleting the App. For information collected by ad providers, use the consent settings above and see each provider's guidance. We do not sell personal information. If you have any questions, please contact us (Section 13).

9. International transfers

Through ad providers and similar services, information may be processed outside Japan.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to prevent the leakage, loss or damage of the information we handle.

11. Children's privacy

The App is not primarily directed at children under 13.

12. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy as needed. We will announce important changes in the App or by other means.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy: Contact form (the form is in Japanese; you may write in English)
