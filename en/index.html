Game Clock (対局時計)

By Miyajin Product

Place your device between two players. Tap your side after each move.

A two-player game clock for shogi, go, chess and other board games, with sudden death, byoyomi and Fischer increment. No account is needed. Game records (names and time used) are stored only on your device and never sent.

Three time controls

Sudden death, byoyomi (shogi style) and Fischer (increment per move).

Ready-to-use presets

Standard time controls for shogi, go and chess, or set your own. Your last choice is remembered.

Big, face-to-face clocks

Face to face in portrait, side by side in landscape. Works on iPhone and iPad.

Never lose your game

Pause, automatic pause, screen stays on, and sounds and haptics.
